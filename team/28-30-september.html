The rules, briefly

  • No conversation closes before the webinar and a private meeting have both been offered. On 18 September we made 43 calls and got zero registrations — only one option had been offered.
  • If they cannot come, write down why: the date, the city, the budget, or indecision.
  • Budgets are already on the record — they declared them on our form. What is missing is area and timing. Take those from every call.
  • No developer or company names. No unit-level prices — say "from €250,000". No competitor names. No project-level Golden Visa doubts.
Why this week matters The Golden Visa threshold in Attica has risen to €800,000, while conversion and restoration projects remain inside the €250,000 exception, and a 15% transfer tax for non-EU buyers is on the table for 2027. The line for the client: the question is no longer where to buy, but which project is still inside that exception — and how the payment is secured.
